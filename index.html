🧭 How every chapter works

  • 🏅Six small stepsEach step ends with something that works, and earns a badge. Stop anywhere and you still have a win.
  • 🔄Two jobs, swap every stepThe Builder plugs in wires. The Coder uploads and changes the code.
  • 🧪Kids change the codeEvery step has a challenge: change a number or a word and see what happens.

🧰 What you need

  • An Arduino UNO starter kit. These guides were written with the ELEGOO Super Starter Kit for UNO. Other UNO kits with the same parts will work too.
  • A computer with the free Arduino IDE and a USB port.
  • A grown-up helper whose main job is to ask "What do you think will happen?"
  • For the finishing touches: a small box, tape, and a USB phone charger or power bank.
💡 Works offline Every guide is a single page with its pictures and code built in. Your checkmarks are saved in your own browser, on your own device.

Made at a kitchen table by a dad and two builders, ages 13 and 10. Free to use, share and remix.
