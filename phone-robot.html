Step 1

Try it on the Mac

⏱ About 10 minutes

A web page needs a server: a program that hands out the page when a browser asks. The Mac has a tiny one built in.

  1. Start the tiny server in Terminal. 
    cd ~/"Claude Projects/Door Greeter Bot"
python3 -m http.server 8000
  2. Open this address in Safari or Chrome on the Mac. 
    http://localhost:8000/phone-robot/
  3. Tap to start, allow the camera, and play a round.

🏆 The win: the robot counts down, reads your hand through the Mac's camera, and tells you who won.

🏅 Badge: Web Wizard

Stop the server with Control + C. Double-clicking the file won't work: the camera and the AI only switch on for pages that come from a server.

🌍
Step 2

Put it on the internet

⏱ About 10 minutes · a parent job

An iPhone only allows the camera on secure pages, the ones whose address starts with https. The Build-a-Bot site on GitHub Pages is one of those. So the phone can't just borrow the Mac's tiny server.

Push the project to GitHub, the same way as every other chapter. A minute or two later, the Phone Robot is live.

🏆 The win: this address opens the start screen on the Mac:

https://keep-code-simple.github.io/build-a-bot/phone-robot/

🏅 Badge: Published

Step 3

Open it on the phone

⏱ About 5 minutes
  1. Get the address onto the iPhone. The easiest way: open it on the Mac, then AirDrop it to the phone or send it in a message.
  2. Open it in Safari.
  3. Tap the giant Tap to start 🤖 button. That one tap does three jobs: it lets the robot speak, turns on the camera, and starts downloading the AI.
  4. When the phone asks about the camera, tap Allow.
  5. Wait for "Loading the robot's brain..." to finish. The first time takes a little while.

🏆 The win: the robot says "Hi! I'm the Phone Robot!" and you see yourself in the corner, mirrored like a selfie.

🏅 Badge: Robot in Your Pocket

💡 Make it look like a real app In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The robot gets its own icon and opens full screen. If the camera acts up that way, play in a normal Safari tab instead.
✌️
Step 4

Play, and test it properly

⏱ As long as you like

Prop the phone up so it can see your hand, about an arm's length away.

  1. Pick a player. Tap a name. Tap and hold a name to change it. Names stay on this phone.
  2. The robot commits first. It locks its move in a sealed envelope 🔒 before the countdown, so it can't cheat.
  3. ROCK... PAPER... SCISSORS... SHOOT! Throw your move on SHOOT and hold it still.
  4. The robot votes. For half a second it checks every camera picture. The move it saw most wins. If it can't tell, it says "I couldn't see that. Again!"
  5. The envelope opens. ✊ fist = rock, ✋ open hand = paper, ✌️ peace sign = scissors. First to 3 wins.
  6. Leaderboard: the longest winning streak for each player is saved on the phone.

🧪 Kid challenges

All of these are near the top of phone-robot/index.html, in the part called SETTINGS. Change it, save, reload the page.

  • 🎭Give it a personality: write your own win, lose and tie lines. Then tap the ⚙️ on the phone and pick the funniest voice, speed and pitch.
  • 🤟Secret gestures: on the "Who is playing?" screen, hold up 👍 and the robot dances. 🤟 makes it say something nice. 👎 and ☝️ do nothing yet. That's your job.
  • 🕵️The cheat switch: change robotCheats: false to true. The envelope shows ❓ instead of 🔒. Can your friends tell it's cheating?
  • 📨Play a friend: send the link to a cousin. The robot runs on their phone too, with their own names and scores.

The line to change for the cheat switch

  robotCheats: true,

A secret gesture you can add

    Thumb_Down: { say: "Boo! Thumbs down to you too!", dance: false },

After changing the code, test on the Mac (Step 1), then push to GitHub so the phone gets the new version. If the phone still shows the old one, pull down on the page to reload it.

🕵️ Troubleshooting

Detective time. Tap a problem to see what to check.

"I can't see!" (the camera is blocked)

In Safari, tap aA in the address bar, then Website Settings, then Camera, then Allow. Reload the page.

The camera doesn't work when I use the Mac's address on the phone

That's the https rule. The phone only allows the camera on secure pages. Use the GitHub Pages address from Step 2.

"No brain yet!"

The robot downloads its AI each time, so it needs internet. Check the Wi-Fi and tap Try again.

The robot doesn't talk

Flip the phone's silent switch off and turn the volume up. Then tap the ⚙️ and press Say something. Try a different voice in the list.

It says "I couldn't see that" a lot

Hold your move still on SHOOT, about an arm's length away, with light on your hand and not behind it. Watch the corner picture: the pink dots show what the AI sees.

The phone still shows the old version after I changed the code

GitHub Pages takes a minute or two after a push. Then reload the page on the phone. If it's on the Home Screen, close it fully and open it again.

On the Mac it says the page can't be found

Is the tiny server still running in Terminal? It must be started from the Door Greeter Bot folder, not from inside phone-robot.

🔐 Privacy

  • The camera picture stays on the phone. The AI runs inside Safari. No pictures are sent anywhere, and none are saved.
  • The page only downloads two things: the AI's code and the hand model, both from Google's public files.
  • Names and scores stay on that phone, in the browser's own storage. The shared files say "Player 1" and "Player 2".
  • No microphone, no tracking, no ads.
